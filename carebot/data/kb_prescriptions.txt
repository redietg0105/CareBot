Title: Prescription Refills

Patients can request a prescription refill through the online patient portal or by calling the clinic's refill line. Refill requests typically take 2-3 business days to process, so patients are encouraged to request refills before their current supply runs out.

Controlled substance prescriptions cannot be refilled automatically and require a follow-up visit with the prescribing provider.

If a pharmacy reports that a prescription is not on file, patients should contact the clinic directly rather than resubmitting the request multiple times, as duplicate requests can delay processing.

This assistant provides general clinic policy information only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or guidance on medication dosing. Questions about symptoms, side effects, or whether a medication is appropriate should always be directed to a licensed provider.
