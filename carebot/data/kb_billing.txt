Title: Insurance, Billing, and Payment

The clinic accepts most major insurance plans, including PPO and HMO plans. Patients should bring a valid insurance card to every visit, as coverage can change between visits.

Co-pays are due at the time of service. Patients without insurance can ask the front desk about self-pay rates and available payment plans.

Billing statements are sent by mail and are also available through the online patient portal. Questions about a specific charge should be directed to the billing department, not the clinical staff.

If a claim is denied, patients will receive a written explanation of benefits from their insurance provider. The clinic's billing team can help patients understand next steps, including appeals.
