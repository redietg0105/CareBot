Title: Scheduling and Rescheduling Appointments

Patients can schedule a new appointment by calling the front desk or using the online patient portal. Same-day appointments are subject to provider availability and are not guaranteed.

To reschedule or cancel an appointment, patients should provide at least 24 hours notice. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a missed-appointment fee, as outlined in the clinic's financial policy.

New patients should arrive 20 minutes before their first appointment to complete intake paperwork. Returning patients should arrive 10 minutes early.

Telehealth appointments are available for follow-up visits and medication check-ins, subject to provider approval. A stable internet connection and a private space are recommended for telehealth visits.
